Frequently asked

  • How do I restore my subscription? Open Ease → Premium → "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the Apple ID used for the purchase.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? On your iPhone: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Ease → Cancel. Your subscription stays active until the end of the paid period.
  • Audio stops when I lock the screen? Make sure Ease is allowed to play in the background and that Silent Mode / Focus isn't muting media.
  • Does Ease work offline? Core sounds and tools work without a connection once the app is installed.
  • Apple Health: Health sync is optional. You can enable or revoke it anytime in the iOS Health app.
